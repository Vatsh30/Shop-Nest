## Project Overview

ShopNest is a clean, modern e-commerce landing page designed to make
online shopping simple and convenient. The page highlights product
categories, trending products, key shopping benefits, and clear calls to
action.

## Figma Design

[Open the ShopNest landing page in
Figma](https://www.figma.com/design/6FF9mNwkoHvLTvPq4XxcEt/Untitled?node-id=0-1&t=rac1dm0hTHRy1M3G-1)

## Design Features

-   Header with brand name, navigation links, search, and a shopping
    call to action.
-   Hero section with a headline, short description, visual, and primary
    button.
-   Product category section.
-   Trending products section with product cards and prices.
-   Feature section highlighting product variety, secure checkout, and
    fast delivery.
-   Footer with supporting information.

## Target Audience

The design is intended for college students, young adults, and general
online shoppers who want a straightforward and convenient shopping
experience.

## Design Choices

-   **Primary color:** Blue (`#2563EB`)
-   **Dark text color:** Navy (`#172554`)
-   **Background:** Light gray (`#F8FAFC`)
-   **Typography:** Arial
-   **Visual style:** Minimal, modern, and easy to navigate.

## Reusable UI Components

The design includes reusable component patterns for:

2.  Product cards.
3.  Feature cards.
